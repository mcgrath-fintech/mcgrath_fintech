## 🚀 Featured Project: Snowflake FinOps Portfolio

**Problem:** Teams struggle to see a clean, credible picture of Snowflake spend and where idle waste hides.  
**Build:** A completed Snowflake + dbt portfolio project with tested cost marts, dbt docs/lineage, department budget variance, forecasts, storage costs, top spenders, and a Streamlit dashboard.  
**Status:** Finished and retired as a standalone product idea after reviewing Snowflake's native cost tooling. The Pro experiment is public, Apache-2.0, and kept as a companion artifact rather than an active paid tier.
